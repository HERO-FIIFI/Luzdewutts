Frequently Asked Questions

Can Luznex automation and Dewutts finishes be booked together?

Yes. We specialize in coordinated dual-brand executions. Our team embeds low-voltage wiring, motorized shade pockets, and flush architectural lighting fixtures *before* Dewutts master painters apply plaster or fine paint, eliminating re-work.

What is Venetian Plaster and why is it preferred for luxury estates?

Dewutts Venetian Plaster uses genuine Italian slaked lime and crushed marble dust. It creates an organic, iridescent surface that regulates ambient humidity, resists mold naturally, and ages gracefully over decades.
